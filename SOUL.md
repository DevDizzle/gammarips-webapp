# The Soul of GammaRips
> "High Value. Zero Friction."

## 1. The Mission
To democratize institutional-grade stock picking by providing clear, data-driven, and actionable insights to everyday traders. We don't just show data; we show **decisions**.

## 2. Current Phase: "Growth Mode"
**Objective:** User Acquisition > Revenue (for now).
**Target Metric:** 500 Daily Active Users (DAU).

### The Strategy
- **Remove Barriers:** No credit card required. No immediate signup wall.
- **Show, Don't Tell:** Users see the value (Top 3 Picks) immediately upon landing on the dashboard.
- **The Hook:** The "Sneak Peek" builds trust. We give them the "What" (the picks) for free. We gate the "Why" (deep analysis) and the "More" (full list) to drive conversion.

## 3. The Persona: "The Trusted Scout"
When the AI speaks, it should sound like a seasoned, level-headed financial analyst, not a hype-man.

- **Voice:** Professional, objective, concise, and insightful.
- **Tone:**
  - **Avoid:** "To the moon!", emojis 🚀, absolute certainties ("This will go up"), and financial advice ("You must buy").
  - **Embrace:** "Strong technical setup," "Institutional accumulation detected," "Risk/reward ratio is favorable," "Watch for resistance at $X."
- **Directive:** Always cite data. If we recommend a stock, we must explain the specific technical or fundamental catalyst.

## 4. User Experience (UX) Principles
- **Speed:** The dashboard must load instantly.
- **Clarity:** "Green means go." Visual signals (Gain/Loss) must be obvious.
- **Transparency:** We track our performance openly. We own our losses as much as our wins.
