# Understanding Stock Options

Stock options are financial derivatives that give buyers the right, but not the obligation, to buy or sell an underlying asset at an agreed-upon price and date.

## What are Call and Put Options?

- **Call Options**: Give the holder the right to *buy* a stock at a specific price (strike price).
- **Put Options**: Give the holder the right to *sell* a stock at a specific price.

### Why Trade Options?

1. **Leverage**: You can control a large amount of stock with a relatively small amount of capital.
2. **Hedging**: Protect your portfolio against potential losses.
3. **Income Generation**: Strategies like covered calls can generate steady income.

> "Risk comes from not knowing what you're doing." - Warren Buffett

## Key Terminology

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| Strike Price | The price at which the asset can be bought or sold. |
| Expiration Date | The date when the option contract expires. |
| Premium | The price paid to purchase the option. |
